Bunker (bunkerbio.com) is an incredibly compelling and ambitious concept. Shifting the paradigm of biology to mirror software engineering—where a researcher can simply "upload a molecule, pick an assay, and get results" without high-end lab access or institutional backing—democratizes biotechnology in a powerful way.To turn this landing page into a highly efficient customer conversion engine, here are several key improvements to consider:1. Demystify the "How It Works" (Build Trust Quickly)Biotech founders and independent researchers are inherently skeptical about outsourcing experiments. Right now, the landing page is highly minimalist, which creates an air of mystery but leaves massive operational questions unanswered.Clarify the backend: Is Bunker a network of automated cloud labs (like Emerald Cloud Lab or Strateos), or do you route requests to traditional Contract Research Organizations (CROs)? Clearly stating how the physical science happens builds immediate operational credibility.Define the boundaries: "Upload a molecule" is a broad concept. Specify your supported formats. Do users upload a chemical structure (SMILES string), a genetic sequence (FASTA), or a 3D protein structure (PDB)?2. Explicitly List the Assay CatalogThe phrase "pick an assay" triggers a lot of speculation. Scientists need to know if your capabilities align with their research pipelines before signing up.Create a visual catalog: Instead of keeping the capabilities behind a "Request Access" wall, feature a high-level, scannable list of available assays directly on the homepage.Categorize your offerings: Group them into logical biological buckets so visitors instantly know your scope. For example:In Vitro Binding: Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR), ELISACellular Assays: Cytotoxicity, reporter gene assays, flow cytometryOmics: RNA-seq, targeted proteomics3. Add Transparency to Data Security & IP ProtectionIn biotech, intellectual property (IP) is everything. If an independent researcher uploads a novel, patentable small molecule or an engineered synthetic biology design, they must be 100% certain it will not leak.Provide an IP guarantee: Explicitly state on the landing page that users retain 100% ownership of their uploaded designs and resulting data.Highlight security protocols: Add a brief mention of your security standards (e.g., end-to-end encryption, secure data silos, and strict NDA-compliant laboratory routing).4. Provide Visual Social Proof or MockupsBecause the platform limits immediate access, a user cannot test the software UX right away.Show, don't just tell: Embed a high-fidelity interactive graphic, a looping GIF, or a product walkthrough video showing a user dragging a molecule into a clean dashboard, selecting an assay, and viewing a mock interactive data results screen.Address logistics: Include a small "FAQ" or "How it Works" section that clarifies sample logistics. Do users mail physical compounds to a central repository, or is Bunker strictly handling the digital execution of digital/in silico modeling?
