01 — The approach

Three research fronts. One objective.

Front 01

Map

Full causal maps of how a disease initiates, spreads, and defends itself — from genome to tissue. You cannot eradicate what you cannot survey.

Front 02

Intercept

Therapeutics aimed at the causal pathway itself — the point where progression can be cut, not merely slowed. Curative intent from day one.

Front 03

Fortify

Prophylactic platforms — immunological and genetic — that leave a population permanently defended, so eradication holds.

Smallpox proved eradication is possible. It has been treated as a miracle ever since. We treat it as a method.
02 — The program

From map to eradication.

Fig. 01 — Program cross-section Stage: MAP
01 / MAP

Survey the disease.

Each program begins with a complete causal map of one disease: driver mutations, signaling dependencies, reservoirs, escape routes. Published as we go.

02 / MODEL

Find the breach point.

Computational models of progression identify where the disease is weakest — the intervention points where a single strike changes the outcome, ranked before a molecule is made.

03 / INTERVENE

Build the intervention.

Therapeutic candidates engineered against the ranked breach points, advanced through preclinical and clinical development with curative endpoints — not symptom scores.

04 / ERADICATE

Hold until zero.

Deployment paired with surveillance until incidence reaches zero and stays there. A program closes only when the disease does.

Methodology

We commit to a disease before we commit to a modality. Programs are selected by a written eradication thesis: the causal map, the ranked breach points, the surveillance plan, and the criteria under which we would stop.

Maps and models are published as they are built. Claims stay attributable: every number on this page carries its source, and program pages will carry their data.
