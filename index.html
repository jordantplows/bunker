Live to 100

Bunker is an AI biotech lab built around a single question: what would it take to make a healthy hundred-year life the default outcome, not the exception?

We use machine learning to map how diseases start, spread, and entrench themselves at the molecular level — building causal models detailed enough to reveal where each disease is weakest. Then we design interventions aimed at those precise points, with the goal of prevention and cure rather than management.

The premise is simple. Most disease is not random. It follows patterns — genetic, metabolic, environmental — that can be mapped, modeled, and intercepted before they do irreversible damage. The tools to do this at the scale and speed required are only now becoming available.
